01 · Mark

Construction

Four steps. Same figure at every scale. Radius stays zero.

  1. Outer tetra Equilateral triangle. You are looking down the apex onto the base.
  2. Medial face Connect the three midpoints. The inverted triangle is the inner tetra.
  3. Centroid rays Center to every vertex and every midpoint. Four faces, unfolded in place.
  4. Corner nest Repeat the construction in each corner triangle. That is Tetrahedron G.

04 · Chrome

One top. Dock. Footer.

Glass only on bar, the late corner, dock, and footer. Scroll: the bar smokes out (width + opacity). The top-left triangle appears only when the bar is fully gone. Click it to bring the bar back. Click the bottom-right triangle or reach the end to raise the footer.

Top bar
width + opacity + blur

Full width. Smokes left until fully gone.

Top-left tri
polygon(0 0, 100% 0, 0 100%)

~120px. Appears at the same instant the bar hits zero. Not a morph.

Dock tri
polygon(100% 100%, 0 100%, 100% 0)

Always visible. Clear hypotenuse. Opens the footer.

07 · Controls

Gold sweep

Hover a button. A 45° gold line starts immediately from the bottom-right and runs all the way off the top-left at 80% opacity. No park. Glow appears instantly outside the button. Mouse out: the glow snaps off.
